abstract: >-
  A dual-strategy planner for autonomous inspection of building structures: layered MST-DFS for
  traversing structured space, with collision-aware A* replanning when the structure blocks the tour.
  Evaluated in AirSim on real-world building models, generating paths over 5x faster with up to 14%
  shorter routes, both fully collision-free.
